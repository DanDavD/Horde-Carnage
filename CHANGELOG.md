# Changelog

This file lists the notable changes to the project. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).
Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/) while the game is a prototype (see [CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md#releases)).

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- **Gamepad combat controls and aim assist** (`GamepadAim.cpp`, `PlayerInput.cpp`, `Game.cpp`): the right stick aims with a crosshair held 260 world units ahead of the hunter and soft aim assist toward the boss and her living hairballs within a 48-degree cone; holding `RT` while releasing the right stick keeps the current aim heading while kiting. `RT` fires, `LT` or `RB` dashes, `X` (Square) drinks a flask, and `Y` (Triangle) switches guns. Keyboard, mouse and gamepad work together without switching modes. Covered by `GamepadAimTests`.
- **Settings and controls** (`Settings.cpp`, `SettingsStore.cpp`, `Menu.cpp`, `MenuDraw.cpp`, `PlayerInput.cpp`, `Game.cpp`): Settings opens from the title and the pause, with master volume (0 to 10, squared so the steps sound even), screen shake (off, low or full), display (fullscreen or a window) and frame rate (vsync, 60, 120, 144 or unlimited). A and D or left and right change a value, a click on its line raises it and on its left arrow lowers it. Each change takes effect at once and is saved to `settings.txt` next to the game, or to the page's local storage in a browser, where display and frame rate are left out. A Controls page lists what each key and button does. `--windowed` still opens one run in a window without changing the saved display, and the borderless fullscreen window is no longer kept above other windows. Covered by `SettingsTests` and `MenuTests`, explained in `docs/SETTINGS.md`.
- **Title screen, pause and end screen choices** (`Menu.cpp`, `Session.cpp`, `MenuDraw.cpp`, `Backdrop.cpp`, `EndScreen.cpp`, `PlayerInput.cpp`, `Game.cpp`, `web/shell.html`): the game opens on a title over the darkened arena, with Begin and Quit. Esc (or Start) pauses: the fight holds still and shows behind the menu, blurred by halving it three times and stepping back to 94%, with Resume and Return to title. YOU DIED and VICTORY end in a row of choices, Try again (or Fight again) and Return to title, which light up after the restart delay; R still retries at once. The menus are words in capitals, the selected one between a line and a diamond on each side, and work with keys, the mouse and the d-pad, with a tick and a knock synthesized for them. `Session` gains the `Title` and `Paused` states; going back to the title starts the attempts over. In a browser, losing the pointer lock mid-fight pauses, and the pointer is only locked while playing. Covered by `MenuTests` and `SessionTests`, explained in `docs/MENUS.md` and `docs/GAME_FLOW.md`.
- **Sound** (`Synth.cpp`, `SoundLibrary.cpp`, `SoundCue.cpp`, `SoundBank.cpp`, `World.cpp`, `Boss.cpp`, `Game.cpp`): every sound of the fight is synthesized in code at start-up from a recipe of up to four layers (sine, square, saw, triangle or noise, with pitch slides, envelopes, vibrato, a sweeping low-pass and a soft clip), and played from the world's events: 23 sounds for the guns, the shield, hits, the dash, the flask, the boss's landings, crashes, roar, collapse and death, spit, and a warning for each attack. New events `AttackStarted`, `StrandsSpat` and `FlaskDrunk` carry what had no event before. Each play wanders a little in pitch and volume, leans toward its side of the screen and fades off screen; up to eight voices per sound share one copy of the samples. Covered by `SynthTests`, `SoundCueTests` and `WorldEventTests`, explained in `docs/AUDIO.md`.
- **The game on the heap in the browser** (`main.cpp`, `src/CMakeLists.txt`): with the sounds, the `Game` grew past the browser's 64 KB stack and the web build crashed on its first frame. It is now created with `std::make_unique`, and the web stack is 1 MB. Checked in a browser; explained in `docs/WEB_BUILD.md`.
- **Health bars that show the hit, and the boss's death** (`TrailingBar.cpp`, `Hud.cpp`, `ArenaDraw.cpp`, `BossDraw.cpp`, `Session.cpp`, `Game.cpp`): a hit leaves a pale gold trail where the bar was, which waits 0.6 s while the hits keep coming and then drains to the bar, on the player's bar and the boss's; the boss's bar also shows the damage of the current run of hits. The screen's edges flash red when the player is hit and beat like a heart below 30% health. The boss dies on screen over 1.4 s, flashing, convulsing and screaming as the colour drains out of her, then lying grey and flat, and the VICTORY screen waits 1.5 s for it (restart now waits for the death and the 1 s delay). Covered by `TrailingBarTests` and `SessionTests`, explained in `docs/GAME_FEEL.md` and `docs/GAME_FLOW.md`.
- **Particles** (`Particle.cpp`, `ParticleEmitter.cpp`, `ParticleDraw.cpp`, `WorldEvent.h`, `World.cpp`, `Game.cpp`, `WorldDraw.cpp`): a 512-slot `ObjectPool` of visual particles owned by `Game` with its own PCG32 generator, so throwing particles never allocates memory or touches `World::random`. Each shot throws muzzle sparks, gunsmoke and a brass casing that bounces on the floor and lies there for a few seconds; bullets spark blue or orange off the shield and shatter it into spinning shards on a break; hits on the exposed boss tear dark blood and locks of hair; dashes kick up dust, leap landings send out an expanding ring of stone dust, killed hairballs burst into lighter brown hair, and the boss sheds hair continuously in Phase 2 and faster in Frenzy. Covered by `ParticleTests` and `ParticleEmitterTests`, explained in `docs/GAME_FEEL.md`.
- **Hit stop and screen shake** (`WorldEvent.h`, `World.cpp`, `HitStop.cpp`, `ScreenShake.cpp`, `ImpactFeel.cpp`, `Game.cpp`, `View.cpp`): the world now records what happened in each step (shots, hits on the player and the boss, the shield struck and broken, kills, landings, crashes into walls, roars, the end of each phase) as events. The game reads them after each step: a hit on the player, a break, a kill or the end of a phase drops the fight to 8% of its speed and eases it back to full speed over 0.1 to 0.8 s, the stronger one winning (a first version froze the world for whole steps, which read as lag spikes), and most of them add trauma to a screen shake that is trauma squared, read from smooth noise, wears off in under a second and never moves the HUD or the aim. Shots and bullets on the boss neither slow nor shake. Covered by `WorldEventTests`, `HitStopTests`, `ScreenShakeTests` and `ImpactFeelTests`, explained in `docs/GAME_FEEL.md`.
- **The Matriarch and her hairballs** (`HairBody.cpp`, `BossDraw.cpp`, `Shapes.cpp`, `WorldDraw.cpp`): the boss is a matted mass of hair that breathes, with uneven locks, a sheen toward the torch, and glowing slit eyes. The second phase bristles, turns its eyes red and grows a mouth full of teeth that opens as it roars, howls and spits; the frenzy bristles all over. The body squashes and stretches with every attack. The hairballs share the drawing, with their mother's eyes, and her strands fly as spinning, spiky clumps of hair. Explained in `docs/ART.md`.
- **Stone crypt arena** (`StoneFloor.cpp`, `ArenaDraw.cpp`, `Art.cpp`): the flat floor and its grid are now a floor of stone slabs with bevels, cracks, grain and old blood stains, laid once from a fixed seed by `LayStoneFloor`, with near-black walls of stone blocks around it. Everything is drawn in code. Covered by `StoneFloorTests`, explained in `docs/ART.md`.
- **Torchlight and shadows** (`ArenaDraw.cpp`): the player carries the only light. The floor darkens with the distance from them, a warm glow flickers around them, and every body throws a soft shadow away from them. Warnings, bodies and projectiles are drawn over the dark, so nothing that can hurt is hidden. The corners of the screen are darkened. Explained in `docs/ART.md`.
- **Tricorn hunter** (`PlayerDraw.cpp`): the player is drawn from above as a monster hunter in a tricorn hat, with a long coat, a torn cape that trails behind their run and sways, and a bandolier of brass cartridges. Both hands are on the gun: the pistol held out in front, the SMG like a rifle. The gun not in hand hangs on the hunter, slung across the back or holstered on the hip. Explained in `docs/ART.md`.
- **Cinzel and a new HUD** (`cmake/Dependencies.cmake`, `cmake/EmbedFile.cmake`, `Hud.cpp`, `EndScreen.cpp`, `Text.cpp`): the Cinzel typeface (SIL Open Font License 1.1), downloaded at a pinned commit, checked against its hash and compiled into the executable, for "YOU DIED", "VICTORY", the boss's name and the HUD. The bars are thinner, framed, and without numbers; text is the colour of old bone. The license is copied next to the executable. Explained in `docs/ART.md`.
- **Fullscreen at the screen's resolution** (`ScreenFit.cpp`, `Game.cpp`, `main.cpp`, `web/shell.html`): the game always runs fullscreen, as a borderless window at the monitor's resolution and refresh rate, and in a browser the canvas follows the page. It is laid out for 1280x720 and drawn at the real resolution through scaled cameras, with black bars on screens that are not 16:9, so it is sharp and everyone sees the same arena. The mouse is converted back to game pixels. `--windowed` opens a window that can be resized. Covered by `ScreenFitTests`, explained in `docs/SCREEN.md`.
- **Web build** (`CMakePresets.json`, `src/CMakeLists.txt`, `web/shell.html`, `Game.cpp`): the game compiles to WebAssembly with Emscripten 3.1.74 (`cmake --preset web`), so it can be played in a browser tab on itch.io. `Game::Run` hands each frame to the browser through `Game::Frame` instead of looping, and the page scales the 1280x720 game to fit the window while the mouse keeps aiming where the cursor is. A CI job builds it on every push and pull request. Checked by hand in a local server; explained in `docs/WEB_BUILD.md`.

### Fixed
- **Aim in the browser** (`web/shell.html`, `PlayerInput.cpp`, `Game.cpp`): on itch.io the crosshair froze once the fight started, because raylib 5.5 stops following the mouse in a browser when the cursor is hidden. The page now tracks the aim point in game pixels and `ReadAimPoint` reads it, and a click locks the pointer to the game, so the mouse no longer leaves it mid-fight (Esc gives it back). Checked in a browser by moving the mouse to three points during a fight; explained in `docs/WEB_BUILD.md`.

### Changed
- **Esc pauses instead of quitting** (`Game.cpp`): the game quits from the title's Quit or the window's close button. Explained in `docs/MENUS.md`.
- **Longer barrels** (`Weapon.h`): the pistol's from 14 to 18 units and the SMG's from 22 to 30, so the bigger guns of the new hunter end where the bullets leave. Bullets now start a little further out. Covered by `WeaponTests`.
- **Rendering split out of `Game.cpp`** (`src/app/render/`): the drawing code had grown to about 1000 of the file's 1215 lines. It now lives in one file per part of the picture (the world and its draw order, the boss, the player, the hairy body, the HUD, the end screens, the Debug overlay), with every colour in `Palette.h` and the screen fit and cameras in `View`. `Game.cpp` keeps the loop, the input and a short `Draw`. Nothing on screen changes: before and after captures match, apart from the frame counter. Explained in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`.
- **Warnings for GCC and Clang** (`cmake/Warnings.cmake`): `-Wno-missing-field-initializers`, because Clang flags every designated initializer that names only some fields, which is how the code sets its numbers. Every other warning is still an error.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-25

The whole boss fight, playable from the first attack to "YOU DIED" or "VICTORY".

### Added
- **Build setup** (`CMakeLists.txt`, `CMakePresets.json`, `cmake/Dependencies.cmake`): a CMake project in C++20 that downloads raylib 5.5 and doctest 2.4.11 at configure time, pinned to those releases, so a fresh clone builds with one command. Presets for Debug and Release.
- **Strict warnings** (`cmake/Warnings.cmake`): `/W4 /WX /permissive-` on MSVC (and `-Wall -Wextra -Wpedantic -Werror` elsewhere) for the project's own targets only, so third-party code does not break the build.
- **Fixed-timestep loop** (`FixedTimestep.h`, `FixedTimestep.cpp`, `Game.cpp`): the game simulates at a fixed 60 Hz and renders once per frame, so gameplay does not depend on the monitor's refresh rate. Long frames are clamped to 0.25 s to avoid a spiral of catch-up steps. Covered by `FixedTimestepTests`.
- **Game window** (`Game.h`, `Game.cpp`, `main.cpp`): a 1280x720 window with vsync and a placeholder title screen whose bobbing title is driven by simulation time.
- **Tests and CI** (`tests/`, `.github/workflows/ci.yml`): doctest unit tests for the pure logic library `horde_core`, run by CTest, and a GitHub Actions workflow that builds Debug and Release and runs the tests on every push to `main` and every pull request.
- **Docs**: README, CONTRIBUTING, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `docs/ROADMAP.md` and a pull request template.
- **Vector math** (`Vec2.h`): a small 2D vector type for `horde_core`, so the pure logic and its tests do not depend on raylib. Covered by `Vec2Tests`.
- **Movement model** (`Movement.h`, `Movement.cpp`): a `Motion` component and a `StepMovement` function that accelerate toward a target velocity and brake at a stronger rate when the input is released or points against the velocity, so turning back takes 0.175 s instead of 0.2 s. Input is clamped to length 1, so a keyboard diagonal is not faster than a straight line and a half-pushed stick gives half speed. Covered by `MovementTests`, explained in `docs/MOVEMENT.md`.
- **Player in the arena** (`Player.h`, `Game.cpp`): the placeholder title screen is replaced by an arena with a floor grid and walls, and a player circle you can move. The player is drawn between the last two fixed steps, so movement is smooth above 60 Hz. `MotionAt` spawns a mover with both positions set, so its first frame is not drawn from the origin. Covered by `MovementTests`.
- **Arena walls** (`Arena.h`, `Arena.cpp`): `ConfineToArena` keeps a circle inside the arena and cancels only the velocity that points into a wall, so the player slides along walls instead of sticking. Covered by `ArenaTests`.
- **Keyboard and gamepad input** (`PlayerInput.cpp`, `AnalogInput.h`): WASD, the arrow keys, and the left stick and d-pad of the first gamepad. The stick goes through a radial deadzone that keeps diagonals and rescales from zero, so there is no jump in speed. Covered by `AnalogInputTests`.
- **Facing** (`Facing.cpp`, `Player.h`, `Game.cpp`): the player faces the way the input points and keeps that direction when it stops, shown by an eye on the player's edge. It follows the input, not the velocity, so the melee swing on day 4 goes where the player is pushing now. Covered by `FacingTests`.
- **Movement debug overlay** (`Game.cpp`): in Debug builds only, `F1` shows the input, speed, velocity, facing and position, and draws the velocity and facing as lines, so I can tune movement against numbers. Not unit tested; checked in a Debug build.
- **Follow camera** (`FollowCamera.h`, `FollowCamera.cpp`, `Game.cpp`): the arena grows to 2400x1600 and a camera follows the player with an exponential ease, looks ahead along the player's velocity, and stops 64 units past the walls so the walls stay visible. It is stepped in the fixed update and interpolated like the player. The Debug overlay shows its center. Covered by `FollowCameraTests`, explained in `docs/CAMERA.md`.
- **World** (`World.h`, `World.cpp`, `Game.cpp`): all the simulation state (arena, player, enemies, camera) moves into a plain `World` struct, and the whole fixed step into `StepWorld` in `horde_core`. `Game::Update` now only reads the input and calls it, so a full game step can run in a unit test without a window. Covered by `WorldTests`, including a check that the same inputs always give the same world.
- **Hairball enemy** (`Enemy.h`, `Chase.cpp`, `Game.cpp`): six hairballs spawn on a ring around the player and walk straight at it with the same movement and wall systems as the player, slower (160 against 300). They stop when they touch the player, so they do not shake on top of it. Drawn with tufts of hair and eyes that look at the player. The Debug overlay shows the enemy count. Covered by `ChaseTests` and `WorldTests`, explained in `docs/ENEMIES.md`.
- **Body collisions** (`Collision.cpp`, `World.cpp`, `Player.h`, `Enemy.h`): after everyone moves, overlapping bodies are pushed apart by weight (player 4, hairball 1) and lose the speed at which they were closing, like a perfectly inelastic collision. Four passes with the walls after each settle a crowd. Hairballs no longer pile into one behind a running player, one hairball barely slows the player (292 of 300) and a column of eight nearly stops them (51). Covered by `CollisionTests` and four new `WorldTests` cases, explained in `docs/COLLISIONS.md`.
- **Health and contact damage** (`Health.cpp`, `Collision.cpp`, `World.cpp`, `Game.cpp`): the player has 100 hit points and each hairball touch takes 10. After a hit the player is invulnerable for 0.8 s, so a crowd hurts like one hairball instead of six, and the player blinks to show it. A health bar in the top left corner turns pale during the window. Covered by `HealthTests`, a contact test in `CollisionTests` and three `WorldTests` cases, explained in `docs/HEALTH.md`.
- **Game over and restart** (`Session.cpp`, `PlayerInput.cpp`, `Game.cpp`): a small state machine (`Playing`, `GameOver`) around the world. At 0 hit points the world freezes and a game over screen shows the survival time. After 1 s, Enter, R, or A or Start on a gamepad start a fresh world. The frozen world is drawn without interpolation, so it does not shake. Covered by `SessionTests`, explained in `docs/GAME_FLOW.md`.
- **Mouse aim** (`Facing.cpp`, `PlayerCommands.h`, `Game.cpp`): the player faces the mouse cursor, independent of where it walks, like a twin-stick shooter. The app turns the hardware into a `PlayerCommands` struct, so the simulation never reads a device and tests drive it with plain values. A crosshair replaces the system cursor. Covered by `FacingTests` and `WorldTests`, explained in `docs/WEAPONS.md`.
- **Two guns** (`Weapon.cpp`, `Loadout.cpp`, `World.cpp`, `Game.cpp`): a semi-automatic pistol (one shot per click, at most 6 per second) and an automatic SMG (10 per second while held). A press up to 0.15 s early is kept and fired when the gun is ready. Q, 1, 2 or the mouse wheel switch guns, and a panel in the bottom right corner shows the gun in hand. Each shot shows a muzzle flash. Covered by `WeaponTests`, `LoadoutTests` and `WorldTests`, explained in `docs/WEAPONS.md`.
- **Object pool** (`ObjectPool.h`, `World.h`): a fixed-size pool for short-lived objects: live objects packed at the front of a `std::array`, removal fills the gap with the last one. Enemies (64 slots) and bullets (128 slots) live in pools inside the `World`, so shooting, killing and respawning never allocate memory. Covered by `ObjectPoolTests`, explained in `docs/OBJECT_POOL.md`.
- **Bullets** (`Projectile.cpp`, `Weapon.h`, `World.cpp`, `Game.cpp`): each shot spawns a bullet at the muzzle that flies in a straight line with its gun's speed, range, damage and knockback (pistol 10 damage, SMG 6). A swept circle test (`SweepCircle`) finds the first hairball along each step of flight, so a bullet cannot skip through a target and only the nearest of a line is hit. Bullets are gone at the end of their range or at a wall. The muzzle position comes from one function, used by both the simulation and the drawing. Covered by `ProjectileTests`, `CollisionTests`, `ArenaTests` and `WorldTests`, explained in `docs/BULLETS.md`.
- **Shooting hairballs down** (`Enemy.h`, `World.cpp`, `Game.cpp`): hairballs have 30 health, so 3 pistol or 5 SMG bullets kill one. A hit flashes it white, staggers it for 0.15 s and knocks it back along the bullet. Dead hairballs are removed before contact damage, counted as kills (top right corner and game over screen), and a new ring arrives 2 s after the arena is cleared. Covered by `WorldTests`, explained in `docs/BULLETS.md`.
- **Dash** (`Dash.cpp`, `World.cpp`, `Game.cpp`): Space or left Shift dashes 180 units in 0.12 s toward the movement input, or toward the aim when standing still. The player is invulnerable for 0.15 s, passes through bodies but not walls, cannot fire during the burst (a click is kept and fires right after), and leaves at running speed. 0.5 s cooldown after the burst. All damage to the player now goes through one function, so the dash protects from every source. A trail of fading ghosts and a recharge bar under the health bar. Covered by `DashTests`, `WeaponTests` and `WorldTests`, explained in `docs/ABILITIES.md`.
- **Flasks** (`Flasks.cpp`, `Health.cpp`, `World.cpp`, `Game.cpp`): E drinks one of three flasks per attempt. The charge is spent at once; after 1 s the drink heals 40, up to the maximum. While drinking the player moves at 35% speed and cannot dash or shoot, and a hit wastes the flask. Drinking at full health is refused. Bottle icons next to the health bar and a ring around the player during a drink. Covered by `FlasksTests` and `WorldTests`, explained in `docs/ABILITIES.md`.
- **The Matriarch** (`Boss.h`, `World.cpp`, `Game.cpp`): the game is now one fight against one boss, a giant hairball in the middle of the arena. For now it walks at the player (120 units/s, slower than a hairball), shoves the player (weight 40 against 4) and takes 20 per touch. It has 1200 health; bullets flash it but neither stagger nor push it, so the SMG cannot lock it in place. A dead boss stays in the world but stops moving, blocking, taking bullets and hurting. Its health bar runs along the bottom of the screen under its name, and the game over screen shows how much health it had left. Covered by `BossTests`, `WorldTests` and `SessionTests`, explained in `docs/BOSS.md`.
- **Death, victory and attempts** (`Session.cpp`, `World.cpp`, `Game.cpp`): the session has three states now: `Playing`, `Dead` and `Victory`. Emptying the boss's bar wins the fight; when the boss and the player fall in the same step, the win counts. "GAME OVER" becomes "YOU DIED" (attempt, time, the boss's health left), and a new "VICTORY" screen shows the attempt that won, the time and the accuracy, from a new count of shots that hit. Both screens fade in over the 1 s restart delay inside a dark band. The attempt number survives a retry, starts over after a win, and shows in the top right corner. In Debug builds `F4` defeats the boss at once. Covered by `SessionTests` and `WorldTests`, explained in `docs/GAME_FLOW.md`.
- **The Charge** (`Boss.cpp`, `World.cpp`, `Game.cpp`): the boss's first attack, and the attack state machine every later attack will share: a warning, the attack and a recovery. After 2 s of stalking the boss crouches for 0.8 s while its path is drawn on the floor; the path follows the player, then locks for the last 0.3 s. It runs at 1000 units/s for up to 800 units, through the player rather than pushing them, and a hit takes 30 and throws the player sideways. A run that ends at a wall stuns it for 1.5 s, one in the open recovers in 0.5 s. The path on the floor comes from the same `PlanChargePath` the run follows. `F1` shows the boss's state in Debug builds. Covered by `BossChargeTests`, `BossTests` and `SessionTests`, explained in `docs/BOSS.md`.
- **The Hair Ring** (`Boss.cpp`, `World.cpp`, `Projectile.cpp`, `Game.cpp`): the boss's second attack, taking turns with the Charge. The boss swells for 0.6 s while a red ring and a pale lane on the floor show the gap, which opens 90 degrees from the player, on one side and then on the other. Then it spits 42 strands (48 directions minus a 45 degree gap) at 220 units/s, each taking 15, and recovers for 0.8 s. The strands are `Projectile`s in a pool of their own (128 slots), so they are only ever tested against the player: a dashing player passes through them, and one that touches the player is spent even inside the invulnerability window. `StepBoss` returns what the world has to spawn instead of writing into its pools. More waves per ring and a gap that turns between them are numbers, ready for the combos and the second phase. `F1` shows the strands in flight in Debug builds. Covered by `BossHairRingTests`, `BossTests`, `ProjectileTests`, `Vec2Tests` and `WorldTests`, explained in `docs/BOSS.md`.
- **Seeded random numbers** (`Random.cpp`, `World.cpp`, `Session.cpp`, `Game.cpp`): a hand-written PCG32 generator, so the same seed gives the same numbers on every compiler. Every `World` has its own generator and seed, and the `Session` hands each attempt the next seed from its own, so attempts differ and a whole run can be replayed. The game seeds the session from the system, and `F1` shows the seed in Debug builds. Covered by `RandomTests` (including the reference PCG32 outputs) and `SessionTests`, explained in `docs/RANDOM.md`.
- **The Leap** (`Boss.cpp`, `Shockwave.cpp`, `World.cpp`, `Game.cpp`): the boss's third attack, after the Charge and the Hair Ring. It crouches for 0.35 s, then spends 1 s in the air, out of reach: no collisions, no bullets, no touch damage. Its shadow follows the player at 200 units/s, slower than the player runs, and locks for the last 0.35 s. The landing hurts a player within 100 units of the shadow for 35, and sends a shockwave that grows at 380 units/s up to 420 units and hurts for 20 once. The wave cannot be outrun, only dashed through, and a player caught by the landing is not hit again by the wave. Then 1 s of recovery. Shockwaves are a component and a pool of their own (8 slots). Covered by `BossLeapTests`, `ShockwaveTests` and `BossTests`, explained in `docs/BOSS.md`.

- **Attack choice** (`AttackChoice.cpp`, `Boss.cpp`): the boss picks its next attack at random when a stalk ends, weighted by the distance to the player (up close the Hair Ring, in between the Leap, far away the Charge), and never the attack that just ended. The fight still opens with a Charge. Every warning is stretched or shortened by up to 15% at random, while the Charge's aim lock and the Leap's shadow lock keep their exact length, so dodging to a rhythm fails but watching the boss always works. Every pick draws from the world's seeded generator. `F1` shows the weights at the current distance in Debug builds. Covered by `AttackChoiceTests` and `BossComboTests`, explained in `docs/BOSS.md`.
- **Combos** (`AttackChoice.h`, `Boss.cpp`): 35% of the Charges and Leaps chain into a second attack that starts in place of the first one's recovery, with a warning 0.7 times as long and a recovery of at least 1.2 s after it. Three combos: the Rebound (Charge, Charge), the Pursuit (Charge, Leap) and the Crash (Leap, Hair Ring). Covered by `AttackChoiceTests` and `BossComboTests`, explained in `docs/BOSS.md`.
- **Cause of death** (`DamageSource.h`, `World.cpp`, `Game.cpp`): `HurtPlayer` records the source of every hit that lands in `World::lastHitBy`, and the death screen names it: "Attempt 4. Slain by her Leap after 0:23." In a fight learned by dying, that is the lesson. Covered by `BossTests`, `WorldTests` and `SessionTests`, explained in `docs/GAME_FLOW.md`.

- **Alternating shield** (`Shield.cpp`, `Boss.cpp`, `World.cpp`, `Game.cpp`): the boss's bullets meet a shield before its health. The blue Shell (150) bounces bullets under 8 damage, so only the pistol breaks it; the orange Barrier (100) takes at most 6 per bullet and grows back at 40 a second, so only the SMG breaks it. A break staggers the boss for 1 s, cutting off its attack, and leaves it exposed for 5 s, then the other kind comes up. A band around the boss and a bar under its health show the shield; `F2` breaks it in Debug builds. Covered by `ShieldTests`, `BossTests` and `SessionTests`, explained in `docs/SHIELD.md`.

- **Second phase** (`Boss.cpp`, `World.cpp`, `Game.cpp`): emptying the first bar no longer wins. The boss transforms for 2 s, invulnerable, with its bar filling back up, then roars a 300 unit shockwave and starts a second phase with red eyes, shorter stalks (0.6 to 1.2 s) and warnings (0.75 to 1.0), combos on half of its Charges and Leaps, 4 s openings in its shield, and the Aimed Volley. Only the second bar wins. `F3` starts the transformation in Debug builds. Covered by `PhaseTwoTests` and `SessionTests`, explained in `docs/BOSS.md`.
- **The Aimed Volley** (`Boss.cpp`, `World.cpp`, `Game.cpp`): the second phase's new attack. After a 0.55 s warning with a line on the floor toward the player, three bursts 0.3 s apart, each five strands 8 degrees apart at 520 units/s for 12 each. The first and third go where the player is, the second where the player will be if they keep running, so neither running in a straight line nor stopping beats it. The death screen names it. Covered by `BossVolleyTests` and `PhaseTwoTests`, explained in `docs/BOSS.md`.

- **Frenzy** (`Boss.cpp`, `World.cpp`, `Game.cpp`): at half of its second bar the boss roars again and falls into a frenzy, with a bristling red look. Its Charge throws a pair of strands every 0.15 s while it runs, one at the player and one ahead; the Hair Ring comes as two rings with the gap turning 30 degrees; the Leap's shadow follows at 240 units/s and its landing spits a ring of 12 strands; the Volley fires five bursts. Stalks drop to 0.4 to 0.9 s and 65% of the Charges and Leaps open a combo. `F3` starts it in the second phase in Debug builds. Covered by `FrenzyTests`, explained in `docs/BOSS.md`.

- **The Summon** (`SpawnMarker.cpp`, `Boss.cpp`, `World.cpp`, `Game.cpp`): the second phase's last attack. The boss howls for 0.75 s while four marks appear on the floor 250 units around the player, turned at random and kept inside the walls, never on the player or the boss; 0.75 s later a hairball comes out of each, shakes itself for 0.3 s and chases, and spits a strand at the player every 3 s (for 8), out of step with the others, swelling red just before. Eight hairballs alive at most; six marks in a frenzy. It is picked most when the player is far away, so distance is no longer a safe answer. The marks are a component and a pool of their own (16). Covered by `SummonTests` and `PhaseTwoTests`, explained in `docs/BOSS.md`.

### Changed
- **Static C++ runtime** (`CMakeLists.txt`): the executable links the C++ runtime in, so the release zip runs on a Windows PC that does not have the Visual C++ Redistributable installed. The game no longer needs `VCRUNTIME140.dll` or `MSVCP140.dll`.
- **Stalking between attacks** (`Boss.h`, `Boss.cpp`): the fixed 2 s between attacks becomes a random 0.8 to 1.6 s, with only the first stalk of a fight still 2 s, so there is less free time to shoot and no countdown to learn. `Boss::attackInterval` is gone. Covered by `BossChargeTests`, `BossHairRingTests`, `BossLeapTests` and `BossComboTests`.
- **Arena and camera** (`World.h`, `World.cpp`): the arena shrinks from 2400x1600 to 1640x1040, so the boss is never far, and the camera follows a point between the player and the boss (40% of the way, at most 200 units from the player), so the boss stays in view without pushing the player off screen. The player starts 400 units south of the boss, facing it. Covered by `BossTests` and `WorldTests`, explained in `docs/CAMERA.md`.
- **Plan** (`README.md`, `docs/ROADMAP.md`): the game turns from a horde shooter into one hard boss fight in the style of Gun Bros and Dark Souls 1. Waves, XP, levels, power-ups and new guns leave the plan; the first release waits until the whole fight works.

### Removed
- **The hairball ring** (`World.cpp`): the fight no longer starts with six hairballs around the player, a cleared arena no longer gets a new ring, and kills are no longer counted. The hairball itself stays, for the boss's summons in its second phase, and its tests now build their own crowds.
